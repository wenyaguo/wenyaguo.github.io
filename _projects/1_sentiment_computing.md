My research in **Sentiment Computing** focuses on understanding human sentiment, opinions, and affective information from complex and multimodal data.

I am particularly interested in multimodal sentiment analysis, aspect-based sentiment understanding, cross-domain sentiment analysis, and the integration of textual and visual information for affective computing.

Representative research topics include:

- Multimodal sentiment analysis
- Aspect-based sentiment understanding
- Cross-domain sentiment learning
- Multimodal representation learning
- Sentiment understanding with large language models
