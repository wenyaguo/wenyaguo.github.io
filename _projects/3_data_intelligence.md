My research in **Data Intelligence** focuses on developing intelligent methods for learning, reasoning, and decision-making from complex, heterogeneous, and evolving data.

I am particularly interested in combining data-centric learning, knowledge representation, foundation models, and intelligent agents for real-world applications.

Representative research topics include:

- Data-centric artificial intelligence
- Knowledge-enhanced reasoning
- Time-series intelligence
- Knowledge graphs
- Foundation models for complex data
- AI agents and intelligent data analysis
