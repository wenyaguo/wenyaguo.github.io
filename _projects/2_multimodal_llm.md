My research in **Multimodal Large Language Models** focuses on enabling foundation models to understand, reason over, and generate multimodal information.

I am interested in combining large language models with visual and structured information to improve multimodal understanding and reasoning.

Representative research topics include:

- Multimodal large language models
- Vision-language understanding
- Multimodal reasoning
- Knowledge-enhanced multimodal learning
- Multimodal generation
- Efficient adaptation of foundation models
